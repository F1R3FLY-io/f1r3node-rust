# P2-06 decisions: settlement and commitment release

Patch P2-06, rev-stacks patch plan r7.2. The patch moved to lane B on
2026-10-30. Branch `patch/P2-06`, cut from `patch/P2-15`. All code in this
patch is new. Nothing is ported. The change is in `casper/src/rust/v6/settle.rs`.
The executor is not wired (see P2-05: the wiring waits for `patch/P1-04b`).
`settle_block` is the one call that the executor makes after the merge.

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | `settle_block` runs, in order: the charges (P2-05, P2-15), the release, the native debits and deposits in the canonical form, the REV hook, the conservation check, then the checkpoint. | — | Design §6.11 step 4, and Decision 31 (the hook runs after settlement, before the conservation check). | — |
| 2 | Release: `Settlement.released[w]` is the block's commitments on `w` less what `w` paid. Nothing is written for a release, because the commitment never left the wallet (no escrow, Decision 3). The commitment table belongs to the block's gate and ends with the block. The released amounts go to the record (P2-10). | Write the release. | Design §6.5: "the unspent (Delta - k) × phloPrice never left". | Yes |
| 3 | Every admitted envelope is charged for its run, whether it merged, lost a conflict or failed. A refused envelope pays nothing and runs nothing. | Charge only merged runs. | The decision of October 25: an envelope that loses a merge conflict still pays for its run. | Yes |
| 4 | The REV hook: `HookCall::Noop` does nothing. A `HookCall::Contract` stops settlement with an error. | Run the contract. | Nothing can install a contract hook yet (P2-08 decision 6). Running one needs a metered runtime under the hook authority, which is later work. The error keeps a future hook from being skipped without notice. | Yes |
| 5 | The conservation check on every block is incremental. It reads the balances of every wallet that settlement touches (the payers, the proposer and the compute wallet) before the debits and after the hook. The two sums must be equal, or settlement fails. | Only the arithmetic `debited == credited`. | Design §6.8: the per-block check is incremental. It reads the state, so a defect in the native writes is also caught. The arithmetic check stays in `apply`. | Yes |
| 6 | The full audit is `settle::audit(store, wallets)`: the listed wallets plus the compute wallet plus the legacy bond total (Decision 32) must equal the genesis supply. It takes the wallet list from its caller. | Scan every channel in the store. | The store gives no index of the wallet channels. The occasional full audit needs a wallet index, which is P2-10 / P2-20 work (the record already names each wallet that pays). | Yes |
| 7 | A block that charges nothing keeps the merged root as its settled root. | Always checkpoint. | The canonical form has nothing to write. | Yes |

## Tests

The audit holds at the pre-state and at the settled state, and the supply equals wallets.txt plus the legacy bond total.
