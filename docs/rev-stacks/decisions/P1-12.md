# P1-12 decisions: 0035 configuration, record packing and pacing

Patch P1-12, rev-stacks patch plan r7.7, lane A2. Branch `patch/P1-12`, cut
from `patch/P1-11`. Port of d1f3ab5e1, without the vault-settlement record
parts (brief).

## How 0035 applied

- Clean: `casper_conf.rs` and `casper_launch.rs` (`casper.v6-fallback-distance`, default 8), `node/src/rust/configuration/mod.rs` (0 fails startup), `v6/core.rs` (`DEFAULT_FALLBACK_DISTANCE`), `v6/propose.rs` (`pack_records`: cited records packed in order within both caps).
- `v6/engine.rs`: 0035's record packing, the fallback distance and the floor pacing, with the P1-04b executor. The caps come from a `ShardResourcePolicy` argument (below). M0's `floor_pacing_engaged` takes the node's `proposal_pacing_floor_distance` as a second argument, which the engine reads from the snapshot's shard conf.
- `casper.rs`: the hook passes the fallback distance, as 0035 does, and the shard resource policy (below).
- `defaults.conf`, `config_mapper.rs`, `diagnostics/tests.rs`: M0's fields are kept beside `v6-fallback-distance`, using the `default_*` functions.

Not ported:
- `models/src/rust/cost_protocol_limits.rs`, `phlo_schedule.rs` and its tests: 0035 adds the two record caps to costacc's `ProtocolCaps` and its genesis policy encoding. On this branch the caps already live in P1-03's `ShardResourcePolicy` (`block_records`, `block_record_bytes`), stored in the protocol-6 genesis.
- `defaults.conf` `casper.genesis-block-data.protocol-v6` and `protocol-caps`: the genesis-side configuration of the policy belongs with the shard-wide v6 switch in the hook round (P1-03 decision B, D-P1-01h-2).
- `casper/tests/v6_executor_spec.rs` (the record-size measurement), `casper/tests/v6/offered_admission_spec.rs` (the pool cap on offered envelopes) and `test_node.rs`: the integration branch's harness and settlement executor. The measurement tests carry over in P1-14.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | The hook reads the shard resource policy from the approved block's post-state (`v6::genesis_policy::read_genesis_policy`) and passes it to `V6Engine::new`. The engine's validity limits come from it. If the state holds no policy, the defaults are used and a warning is logged. | Keep `ShardResourcePolicy::default()` in the engine. Fail startup. | The record caps are validity rules fixed at genesis (0035, Oct 26). Every node reads them from the same state. Until the genesis wiring of the hook round lands, test shards have no policy in state, so a hard failure would stop them. | Yes |
| 2 | The v6 proposer applies M0's floor pacing with the node's `proposal_pacing_floor_distance`. | 0035's fixed 128. | M0 made the pacing distance a node setting. | Yes |

## For lane A1

`V6Engine::new` now takes `fallback_distance` and a `ShardResourcePolicy`.
The hook in `casper.rs` reads the policy from the approved block's state.
When the protocol-6 genesis switch lands (D-P1-01h-2), a v6 flag on a shard
whose genesis has no policy could become a startup error.
