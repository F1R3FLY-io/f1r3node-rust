# FX-15 decisions: work under a membrane is gated step by step (lane C)

Branch `patch/FX-15`, lane C, cut from `patch/FX-8b` (aea9b529d). Design r15, plan r8.16. Decisions 86, 89, 90, 91, 93, 94 and 96. The refusal event encoding is lane C's proposal with lane A2's review (`patch/Q-32` e6abd727b).

## Before this branch

A stored receive was a match candidate only if its wallet could pay one cell (D79). After that check, the continuation's work ran without a limit. Settlement then capped the draw on the owner's wallet. The client's run limit also counted the owner's work.

## Answers

- **F48 (substitution into a fired continuation).** It is charged to the continuation's record, not to the firer. The dispatcher evaluates the body inside `seal::within(record, …)`. Substitution happens during that evaluation, and the matcher and the dispatcher make no charges. `region_spec::substitution_into_a_fired_continuation_is_charged_to_its_installer` shows it: a body that substitutes its argument 16 times costs the installer more than an empty body, and the firer's run costs the same for both. No change is needed.
- **F48 in the close (lane A1).** The close showed a dependence that the envelope runs do not have. With the storage rule off (the close, and legacy runs), `handle_result` credited every storage refund of a COMM to the record that fired it. A stored continuation's consume refund depends on the size of its body, so the PoS code that fired it got the refund for storage that the installer had paid: with a wider body, the proposer's close charge fell by 273 phlo. Lane B measured the same effect. `handle_result` now credits each refund to the payer of the stored item, as `left_in_space_match` already does in envelope runs: the consume refund goes to the continuation's record, and a datum's refund goes to the datum's record. A refund of the item that fired the COMM stays with the firer. A zero refund is still charged, so the run's phlo checks do not change. Tests:
  - `seal_wiring_spec::a_storage_refund_at_a_comm_goes_to_the_payer_of_the_stored_item`: the firer's work is the same for a thin and a wide body (194 and 70 phlo before the fix).
  - `close_charge_spec::substitution_into_a_continuation_fired_in_the_close_is_not_charged_to_the_proposer`: needs FX-16's close charge, so it is on `patch/FX-15-close` (FX-15 with FX-16 merged). The proposer's close charge is the same for both bodies, and the installer pays more for the wide one.
- **Lane B (gross or net).** The meter records the gross amount: the sum of the admitted positive charges and reservations. A refund does not lower it. Settlement charges the net work from the work tally.

## Known issues (Decision 88)

1. Retired by decision 14: the event storage after a store is reserved up front.
2. **Known issue.** For a compound owner, a refusal is checked against the first component wallet that fits its drawn amount. A compound with two near-empty components can stop on either one.
3. **Known issue.** A gated charge outside every step scope takes a fixed identity. No test reached this path.
4. **Known issue.** `admits_waiting` no longer skips an unpayable candidate before the spatial match. The veto in `check_commit` comes after the match, which costs one extra match attempt.
