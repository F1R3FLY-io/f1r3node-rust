# FX-15 decisions: work under a membrane is gated step by step (lane C)

Branch `patch/FX-15`, lane C, cut from `patch/FX-8b` (aea9b529d). Design r15, plan r8.16. Decisions 86, 89, 90, 91, 93, 94 and 96. The refusal event encoding is lane C's proposal with lane A2's review (`patch/Q-32` e6abd727b).

## Before this branch

A stored receive was a match candidate only if its wallet could pay one cell (D79). After that check, the continuation's work ran without a limit. Settlement then capped the draw on the owner's wallet. The client's run limit also counted the owner's work.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | `accounting::membrane::MembraneMeter` holds the state of one run: the gross drawn amount for each wallet, the stopped wallets, the refusals, and in replay the logged refusals and the reached steps. The executor makes one meter for each envelope run and installs it on the runtime. `inj` scopes it as a task-local, and spawned tasks keep it. | Keep the state in `CostManager`. | The matcher must also see the state, and one `Matcher` serves all runtimes of a pool. | Yes |
| 2 | A record is gated when its owner is not a payer that the gate committed for the envelope (`EnvelopeFunding::gates`, Decision 93). The client's own work keeps the run's phlo limit (Decision 90). | Gate by envelope. | Decision 93: a seal that resolves at run time to another wallet is gated, also in the client's own envelope. | Yes |
| 3 | Each positive charge under a gated record is admitted against each wallet of the record's purse: the limit for the block (pre-state balance less what the gate holds, as in D79) less the gross amount that the wallet drew in the run. The charge in REV is the phlo times the record's origin price. An admitted charge goes to the work tally of the record and not to the client's phlo state. | Admit against the net amount. | Decision 86, and lane A2's review point 3: the admission uses the gross amount. Refunds do not raise the allowance during the run. | Yes |
| 4 | A refusal stops the wallet at once, in the same lock as the test. Every later step on that wallet is refused and logged. Each event carries the gross amount drawn at the stop. | Stop only the branch. | Decision 86: all work under t stops. Because the stop is atomic, all events of one wallet carry the same amount. | Yes |
| 5 | A refused step returns `InterpreterError::MembraneStopped`. The error ends its branch. `aggregate_evaluator_errors` drops it, so the run does not fail. The error keeps its kind when it passes through the space as an `RSpaceError`. | Fail the run. | The client pays nothing for t's work and does not fail because of it. | Yes |
| 6 | Step identity (Decision 91, with lane A2's change): `Blake2b256(base ‖ position)`. The base hashes the domain, the source, the content and the random state. For an evaluation, the content is the term being evaluated and the random state is its branch's state. A fired body has the source `Comm` with the merged state. A re-stored item has the source `Restore` with the item's own state. The position counts every gated charge of the scope, also when no identity is computed. | Count positions only when an identity is needed. | A test showed that play and replay disagreed when the position advanced only on demand. | Yes |
| 7 | The cells of a COMM are admitted together in `Matcher::check_commit`, after the spatial match and before the commit. If any gated wallet cannot pay, the COMM is vetoed, and each unpayable record gets a `Cell` refusal. The identity of that refusal is the COMM's merged random state. Play draws the cells there. Replay draws them where the COMM is accounted (`left_in_space_match`, or `handle_result` when the storage rule is off). | Keep D79's `admits_waiting` filter. | `check_commit` sees the continuation's record and the records of all matched data, so a join pays for all its surfaces at once. `admits_waiting` is now the default (`true`), and D79 decision 9 is superseded. | Yes |
| 8 | A charge in a restore scope (a persistent datum or continuation that is stored again, or a peeked datum that is produced again) is never refused and draws nothing. The same applies to the post-COMM charges in `handle_result`. They go to the work tally only. | Admit them like other charges. | A refused restore deletes a persistent contract, and the merge then finds a consume without its join (seen in a test). These charges are storage bookkeeping. Most of them are refunded. | Yes |
| 9 | Replay admits each step that is not logged, and refuses exactly the logged steps. Replay does not stop t on its own meter (lane A2's review). `accept_metered` then checks lane A2's conditions for each run: each logged step was reached; for each stopped wallet, D ≤ A_t, D ≤ W_t, all events carry the same D, and at least one event has A_t − D < c; for a wallet that did not stop, W_t ≤ A_t. | — | Decision 89 and lane A2's table. | Yes |
| 10 | `EnvelopeExecutor::replay_with_refusals` takes the logged refusals for each run. `replay` passes none until lane A2's deploy-log event (Decision 91) carries them. `ExecutedBlock.metered` gives each run's refusals, gross drawn amounts and reached steps. | — | Lane A2 owns the encoding in the deploy log and in the record. | Yes |
| 11 | The close (Decision 96): `CloseFunding` gates each stored record that the close fires. The limit is the block's limit less what the envelope runs drew, taken from `metered`. The close's own PoS code has no record, so it is not gated. Lane A2 installs the meter through `RuntimeManager::compute_state`. | Gate the close's code against the proposer. | Decision 96 withdrew that. Lane B charges it after settlement (FX-16). | Yes |

## Tests

- `rholang` `membrane::tests`: the stop at the first unpayable step, the step identity, a replay that refuses exactly the logged steps, the cells of a COMM, and restore scopes.
- `rholang` `seal::tests::funding_is_scoped_and_spawned_tasks_keep_it`, `errors::tests::a_membrane_stop_keeps_its_kind_through_the_space`.
- `casper` `v6::surface::tests::the_close_gates_every_stored_record_against_what_the_runs_left`.
- `v6::region_spec::work_under_an_underfunded_installer_stops_and_the_client_pays_nothing_for_it`. A process wallet's service loops 300 times:
  - With room of 200 REV, the loop stops. The client's run does not fail, and the client pays what it pays when the service is fully funded.
  - Eight replays with the logged refusals give the same root, drawn amounts and settlement.
  - A replay without the refusals does not give the same root. A replay with a forged drawn amount fails.
- `v6::region_spec::data_sent_to_an_owners_contract_runs_under_the_owners_membrane_and_is_paid_there` (lane B's test): the client's run now counts only the client's own work.

## Known issues (Decision 88)

1. **Known issue.** When a gated produce stores its datum and nothing matches, the `event_storage_cost` charge comes after the datum is stored. If that charge is refused, the datum stays and the charge is unpaid. The amount is one event charge, and no authority is involved.
2. **Known issue.** For a compound owner, a refusal is checked against the first component wallet that fits its drawn amount. A compound with two near-empty components can stop on either one.
3. **Known issue.** A gated charge outside every step scope takes a fixed identity. No test reached this path.
4. **Known issue.** `admits_waiting` no longer skips an unpayable candidate before the spatial match. The veto in `check_commit` comes after the match, which costs one extra match attempt.
