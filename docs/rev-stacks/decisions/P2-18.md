# P2-18 decisions: annotation syntax, normalizer and model

Patch P2-18, "Annotation syntax: normalizer and model", rev-stacks patch plan
r6.4, lane C. Branch `patch/P2-18`, cut from `patch/P2-17`. The normalizer is
ported from `feature/cost-accounted-rho` at `1b5a92f13`
(`rholang/src/rust/interpreter/compiler/normalizer/cost_accounting/`). The
model plumbing is partly ported and partly new code on this base.

Ruling of 2026-10-30: P2-18 goes ahead at the larger size on the
`17d2f98` parser pin. The larger size includes the surface that the plan row
does not name. The section "Extra surface" lists it.

## Extra surface

The plan row names the normalizer and the protobuf model. The ruling asks for
a log of the surface beyond that. This patch changes these places:

- **Equality and hashing.** `models/src/lib.rs`: `PartialEq` and `Hash` for the
  four new messages and the signature oneof. `Par` and `ReceiveBind` compare
  and hash the new fields.
- **Binary encoding.** `models/src/rust/par_serde.rs` and `models/build.rs`
  (decision 13). This is the surface with consensus effect. Without it, every
  state hash changes.
- **Sorter.** New `cost_accounting_sorter.rs`. `ParSortMatcher` and
  `ReceiveSortMatcher::sort_bind` sort and score the new fields. New score
  constants 310 to 317 in `score_tree.rs`.
- **Constructors and helpers.** `Par::append`, `Par::clone_with_exprs`,
  `vector_par`, `concatenate_pars`, `rho-pure-eval` `concatenate` and `eval`.
  The single-value checks of `rho-pure-eval`, of the matcher guard and of the
  reducer treat a `Par` with cost terms as not a single value.
- **Substitution.** `substitute.rs`: `Par`, `ReceiveBind` and the new
  messages.
- **Matcher.** `spatial_matcher.rs`, `match_pars.rs`, `sub_pars.rs`,
  `has_locally_free.rs` (the new messages, and the signature of a bind in its
  free variables), `exports.rs`.
- **Pretty printer.** `pretty_printer.rs`.
- **Reducer.** One refusal in `eval_inner` (decision 8).
- **Struct literals.** About 200 struct literals of `Par` and `ReceiveBind`,
  most of them in tests, set the new fields to empty values.

## Tests

- Normalizer: `compiler::normalizer::cost_accounting::tests` (sugar,
  flattening, pattern guard, signed joins, substitution of a bound
  signature) and `sugar_properties` (property tests: lollipop equals explicit
  layers, uniform signing equals `s -o s`, chains equal explicit stages).
- Sorter: `cost_accounting_sorter::tests`.
- Encoding: `par_serde::tests`. A property test checks, on 256 generated
  terms, that a `Par` without cost terms encodes in bincode byte for byte as
  the derived encoding did, and that bincode and JSON round-trip. The
  CONS-3 pin `io::verify::tests::cons3_stable_hash_pinned_for_known_pars`
  passes unchanged.
- `rholang/tests/cost_syntax_spec.rs`, renamed from the P2-17
  `cost_syntax_refused_spec.rs` with `git mv`. It checks that the three
  forms normalize, that old terms have empty new fields, that the printed
  form normalizes to the same term, that the reducer refuses each form, and
  that a signed term sent as data is inert.

## Open items

1. **P2-19.** Replace the refusal of decision 8 with funding regions. The gate
   of P2-04 takes the signatures named in signed terms through
   `GateInput.named` (Decision 30). P2-19 must give the gate that list from
   the normalized term, so that the gate can refuse foreign signatures.
2. **Field numbers.** Lane A must keep `Par` fields 13 and 14 and `ReceiveBind`
   field 5 free in P1-03 and P1-06.
3. **Sets.** `Ordering::sort_pars` has the tie behavior that the clone-audit
   commit `60bd40947` (Q-27) fixed for maps. It is out of scope here.
