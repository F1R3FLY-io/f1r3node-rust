# P3-03 decisions: multi-signature wallets

Patch P3-03, "Multi-signature wallets", rev-stacks patch plan r7.10, lane C.
Branch `patch/P3-03`, cut from `patch/P3-02` (`c59b01727`). The brief says
"port from the integration branch". Nothing is ported: the native REV layer
of P2-01 already keys a wallet by a compound owner
(`WalletOwner::Compound`, components flattened and sorted), so a
multi-signature wallet needed only a way to fund it, spend it and charge it.

## Rulings used

- Decision 25 (Q-25): a joint wallet keyed by `s1 (*) s2` itself pays once.
- The design ("Fees, minting, sponsors and multi-signature"): a compound token must be spent in full or explicitly
  split. Weakening is forbidden.
- P3-02: the signers of a deployment are the deployer and its verified
  cosigners.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | The joint wallet of `s1 (*) s2` is the wallet of `WalletOwner::Compound([s1, s2])`, on the channel P2-01 computes for that owner. | A new wallet type. | It exists already, with the same canonical bytes for every order of the components. | Yes |
| 2 | Anyone may fund a joint wallet: a `rho:rev:wallet` transfer whose payee is a tuple of owners (`(*d, *c)`), or a stack literal whose cell is the compound. | Only the components may fund it. | A wallet takes deposits from anyone (design, "Wallets disaggregated from the vault"). | Yes |
| 3 | A joint wallet is spent by a `rho:rev:wallet` transfer whose payer is a tuple of deployer ids. Every component must be a signer of the deployment (P3-02). The transfer names the amount. The rest stays in the joint wallet. | Only spends of the whole balance. | This is the explicit split. It never drops a component's consent, so weakening stays forbidden. | Yes |
| 4 | For a named compound, the gate checks the joint wallet at the block pre-state (`resolve_joint_wallets`). If it holds REV, the joint wallet pays the charge once (Decision 25). If it is empty, each component pays in full (Decision 24). | A marker in the term that selects the joint wallet. | The paper's combined token (Rule 3) is the stack on the compound's channel: if it exists, it pays. The check is a native read at the pre-state, so play and replay agree. | Yes |
| 5 | If a funded joint wallet cannot cover `Delta x phloPrice`, the gate refuses (`InsufficientFunds`). It does not fall back to the components. | Fall back to the components. | One rule per pre-state. A fallback would make the payer depend on the amount. | Yes |
| 6 | **Change to P3-02 decision 6:** the payers of an envelope are the payers of the signatures it names. The deployer pays only for an envelope that names none. | P3-02: the deployer always pays. | With a joint wallet, the P3-02 rule would charge the deployer as well as the joint wallet, so the compound would pay more than once. The same holds for `{% P %}[c1 (*) c2]` without the deployer. P2-19's deployer-only terms and unsigned terms keep their payer. | Yes |
| 7 | `rho:rev:wallet` "find" and "balance" accept a tuple of owners, so a client can read a joint wallet. | — | Same `owner_of` for payees and reads. | Yes |

## Tests

- `v6::region_spec::a_funded_joint_wallet_pays_once_for_its_compound`: the
  joint wallet of alice and carol holds 50,000. A cosigned deployment of
  `{% P %}[d (*) c]` makes one charge, on the joint wallet. alice and carol
  keep their balances, the settlement is balanced, and replay agrees.
- `v6::region_spec::a_joint_wallet_is_spent_only_by_all_its_signers`: a
  deployment by alice cosigned by carol moves 700 from the joint wallet to bob.
  After carol's deployer id leaks to a public channel, alice alone cannot spend
  the joint wallet with it. These assertions read the run's post-root, because
  the v6 merge drops native writes (P2-19, open item 3).
- `v6::region_spec::each_component_of_a_compound_pays_the_full_charge` (P3-02)
  still passes: an empty joint wallet leaves the charge on the components.

## Open items

1. The v6 merge drops native REV writes of a run (P2-19, open item 3). Until
   it is fixed, a transfer to or from a joint wallet inside a deployment does
   not reach the settled root.
2. A client command for cosigning (P3-02, open item 1).
