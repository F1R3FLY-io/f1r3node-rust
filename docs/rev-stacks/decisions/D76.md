# D76 decisions: each surface of a COMM pays (executor side)

Branch `patch/D76`, lane A2, cut from `patch/FX-8b` (c41ccb87b). Design revision 10 §6.3, Decision 76 (plan r8.9). Decision 75 (the installer's purse) is Q-31 and lane B's. This branch keeps the draw behind one function so that lane B can plug in the purse rule.

## Before this branch

`comm_phlo` was the number of COMM events in the run's own deploy log, charged to the envelope's paying wallets. A stored receiver's surface paid nothing.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | At each COMM, the storage-rule path (`left_in_space_match`) records the surfaces of the COMM: the receive's record (`seal::of_continuation`, the record that `consume` stored on the continuation), and one record for each send. A send that triggered the COMM has the running task's record. A send matched as a stored datum has the record that paid for the datum if the run stored it (the storage rule's per-item payer). | Record only the receive and the triggering send. | A join matches several sends, and each is a surface. | Yes |
| 2 | A surface is keyed by its seal: the owner and the origin (envelope and price). The record kind and the `from_data` flag are not part of the key. A COMM counts one cell for each distinct key among its surfaces. | Key by the whole record. | Decision 76: surfaces under one seal pay once. The implicit deployment seal and an explicit `{% … %}[d]` in the same envelope are the same seal. | Yes |
| 3 | A surface with no record is the client's part: code with no record (genesis and system contracts), and a datum from the pre-state. | Refuse, or count it as free. | Decision 59, item 5. | Yes |
| 4 | The run's tally (key to cells) is on the run, in the record entry (field 18), and in `ExecutedBlock.surfaces`. Replay rebuilds the entries and compares them, so a forged tally does not replay. The settlement is compared too. | Keep the tally out of the record. | "Replay must check the attribution." | No: field 18 is part of the record encoding. |
| 5 | Settlement: the client's COMM charge is the number of cells on the envelope's own surfaces (key `None`, or origin equal to the envelope), at the envelope's price, against the gate's commitment, as before. Each foreign surface (stored code from another envelope) is a `SurfaceCharge`: cells times the origin's price, drawn from `surface_purse`. Settlement field 7 records it. | Charge foreign surfaces to the client. | Decision 76 with the origin price. | Yes |
| 6 | `surface_purse(surface)` (`v6/surface.rs`) is the only place that names the wallet a surface charge draws on. Today it is the seal's owner, and a compound owner is each of its components, each charged in full (Decision 24). | — | Q-31: lane B replaces this function with the purse at the surface of the stored code (Decision 75). | Yes |
| 7 | The gate commits nothing for a foreign purse. A surface draw is capped at what the purse holds at the merged state, less the block's other debits and its REV transfers from that wallet. A shortfall is neither drawn nor credited. `SurfaceCharge` records both `owed` and `drawn`. | Fail the block, or charge the shortfall to the client. | A stored contract with an empty purse must not make a block fail to settle, and the client did not agree to pay at another price. Whether a shortfall should refuse the COMM is for Q-31. | Yes |
| 8 | A surface draw on a funded process wallet draws the funders' shares pro rata, as a charge on that wallet does (P3-04, FX-7). | — | The funder ledger must match the wallet. | Yes |
| 9 | A send announces its payer to the storage rule before the datum enters the space, and withdraws the announcement after the produce. `datum_payer` reads the stored payers first and then the announcements. | Look up the payer only after the produce. | A parallel branch can match the datum between the produce and the record of its payer, as in the P2-16 race. The surface then read as the client in one order and as the sender in another, and the record did not replay (`storage_charges_do_not_depend_on_the_interleaving`, `pooled_runtimes_give_the_records_of_fresh_ones`). | Yes |

## Gaps (for Q-31 and lane C)

1. **Data have no record.** Only continuations carry the seal record (FX-2's wrapper). A datum stored by an earlier envelope or block is matched as a client surface. To charge the sender of a stored datum, data need a wrapper like the continuation's.
2. **Before the draw, nothing reserves the foreign purse.** The cap in decision 7 makes the result deterministic. It does not stop a stored contract from serving calls after its purse is empty.
3. **The run's phlo limit and exhaustion** still use the run's COMM count (`comm_phlo` on the run), and the client's charge is capped at the gate's `delta`. Cells charged to foreign surfaces do not count against the client's limit.

## Tests

- `rholang` `accounting::comm_ledger::surface_tests::each_distinct_surface_of_a_comm_is_counted_once`.
- `casper` `v6::surface::tests`: the own/foreign split, canonical encoding, and a compound purse.
- `v6::region_spec::each_surface_of_a_comm_pays_one_cell_at_its_origin_price`: Alice stores a contract at price 1, and Bob calls it at price 2. Bob's tally has his surface and Alice's. Bob pays one cell, and Alice's purse pays one cell at price 1. The settlement balances, and replay gives the same tally, settlement and root.
- `v6::region_spec::surfaces_under_one_seal_pay_once`: a send and a receive in one envelope, once bare and once with the receive in `{% … %}[d]`. Each is one cell to the client.
- `v6::exit_spec::replay_refuses_a_record_whose_surface_attribution_is_forged`.
- `v6::region_spec::a_fired_continuation_draws_on_its_author_not_on_the_firing_region` and `a_seal_sent_as_data_to_an_owners_contract_draws_on_no_wallet`: the owner of a stored continuation that another party fires now pays that surface's cell in the firing block. The expected balances include that charge, and the first test asserts that it is not zero.
- `settlement_spec`: the existing tests pass one client surface for each COMM, so their charges do not change.
