# P3-04 decisions: owners (DR-135) and contracts paying from `# P` (Decision 27)

Branch `patch/P3-04`, lane B, cut from `patch/P2-21` (33b46e430), which is `patch/P2-integration` (3b5a7e90a) plus the canonical journal order.

## Scope of this first commit

The first commit builds the part that every answer to memo M-9 needs:
- the wallet of `# P`;
- the authority of a `# P` seal, which comes from the term itself (P10).

M-9 asks how the wallet pays for P's own work under order-then-execute, which affects charging and consensus. Until the ruling, the gate still refuses an envelope that names `# P` as a foreign signature.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | The wallet of `# P` is `WalletOwner::Unforgeable(Blake2b256("f1r3fly/rev-stack/process/v1" ‖ the encoding of the sorted P))`. | A new `WalletOwner` variant. | The same form as the bond and compute owners. A new variant would change the canonical bytes that lane C's P3-07b also changes. Congruent processes get one wallet. | Yes, before any state holds such a wallet |
| 2 | A seal component `# Q` is admissible on a signed term only when the term's body is Q, compared in sorted form at the same substitution level. The runner's identity plays no part. The other components of a compound still need the deployment's signers. | Accept `# Q` when the deployer signs. | P10: the authority is named in the term. The seal certifies itself, so only P can draw on the wallet of `# P` by this route. | Yes |
| 3 | A `# Q` seal on a receive bind is refused. | Compare against the receive. | A bind has no body for Q to equal. The meaning of a hash seal on a bind belongs to the M-9 ruling. | Yes |
| 4 | `signature_owner` resolves `# P` to its process wallet. A stack cell naming `# P` therefore deposits one unit into that wallet. | — | Anyone may deposit (Decision 27). The per-funder record is held for lane C's `JournalEntry` change (memo H). | Yes |
| 5 | The gate is unchanged. Its static view still gives `# P` as an unresolved name, so an envelope that names it is refused. | Admit a self-sealed envelope now (M-9 option c). | Admission depends on the charging model in M-9. | Yes |
