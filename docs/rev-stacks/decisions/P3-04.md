# P3-04 decisions: owners (DR-135) and contracts paying from `# P` (Decision 27)

Branch `patch/P3-04`, lane B, cut from `patch/P2-21` (33b46e430), which is `patch/P2-integration` (3b5a7e90a) plus the canonical journal order.

## Scope of this first commit

The first commit builds the part that every answer to memo M-9 needs:
- the wallet of `# P`;
- the authority of a `# P` seal, which comes from the term itself (P10).

M-9 asks how the wallet pays for P's own work under order-then-execute, which affects charging and consensus. Until the ruling, the gate still refuses an envelope that names `# P` as a foreign signature.

## Order-independent admission of journaled moves (Decision 48, M-8)

Branch commits after the P3-07b merge. P3-04 already had two commits pushed, so this goes on top of them rather than first; pushed history is not rewritten.

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 10 | A journaled move from a wallet is admitted when its amount alone is at most `balance − held`, where `balance` is the pre-state balance of the wallet in that kind and `held` is the block's REV commitment (0 for another kind). A stack literal is admitted when its total is at most the same bound. The run's other journaled moves are not counted. A mint is not checked here (its authority is the kind's name, P3-07b decision 7). | Net the run's moves at record time (P2-21 decision 2). | Decision 48. The verdict of each move no longer depends on the order in which parallel branches record. | Yes |
| 11 | At the end of a run, before the journal closes, `journal_overdrawn` sums each (owner, kind)'s journaled debits and credits. The run is overdrawn when, for some wallet with debits, `balance + credits < debits + held`. | Check per move only. | Decision 48. The total is a sum, so it is the same in play and replay. | Yes |
| 12 | An overdrawn run gets the new outcome `Overdrawn` (tag 6). It is excluded from the merge, its journal moves nothing, and it still pays its charges. Its rholang status is left as evaluated, and replay rebuilds the deploy with `is_failed` from that status, then computes the same verdict. | Report it as `UserFailure`. | Replay compares the recorded `is_failed` with its own evaluation, and an overdraw is not an evaluation failure, so marking it `UserFailure` would make replay refuse the block. | Yes |
| 13 | Inside a run, `balance` shows the pre-state balance less the visible commitments, and `kindBalance` shows the pre-state balance of the kind. Neither adds the run's journaled moves. | Show the journaled net (P2-21 decision 2, P3-07b decision 14). | A read that includes the journal depends on recording order in the same way that admission did, and its result reaches the program. | Yes |
| 14 | A run can no longer spend REV or tokens that it received or minted in the same run. P3-07b's `the_journal_marks_mints_and_keeps_kinds_apart` is changed to match: a transfer funded only by a mint in the same run is refused. | — | Stated cost of option (a) in M-8. | Yes |

Tests:
- `rev_reserve_spec::an_open_journal_holds_transfers_out_of_the_state`: transfers of 600 and 401 from a balance of 1,000 are each admitted, a transfer of 1,001 is refused, the read shows 1,000, and the journal is overdrawn after the second transfer and not before.
- `gated_executor_spec::parallel_transfers_that_overdraw_together_fail_the_run_in_play_and_replay`: two parallel transfers of 30,000, with 50,000 spendable, give `Overdrawn`. Nothing moves, the charge is paid, and the decoded record replays to the same post-root.

## Interim: a self-sealed envelope pays from its process wallet (Decision 49, option c)

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 15 | When an envelope's whole term is one signed term `{% P %}[# P]` with P closed, the gate takes the wallet of `# P` as its only payer (`GateInput.sealed`). The deployer does not pay, and the seal needs no signer. | — | Until region metering lands, the whole envelope is P's own work only in this shape. | Superseded by region metering |
| 16 | Any other envelope that names `# P` is still refused as a foreign signature. | — | Its charge would include work that is not P's. | Superseded by region metering |
| 17 | `GateInput` gains `sealed` and `with_sealed`. `paying_wallets` returns the sealed owners alone when nothing is named, and otherwise adds them to the named payers. `gate_inputs` compiles the term once and routes a self-sealed envelope to `sealed`. | — | The sealed owner's authority comes from the term (P10), so it skips the signer check. | Yes |

Tests: `process::tests::only_a_lone_closed_self_sealed_term_is_a_self_sealed_envelope`, `gate::tests::a_self_sealed_envelope_is_paid_by_its_process_wallet_alone`, and `gated_executor_spec::a_self_sealed_envelope_is_paid_from_its_process_wallet_in_play_and_replay` (Alice holds no REV; the `# P` wallet, funded in state, pays every charge; replay gives the same post-root).
