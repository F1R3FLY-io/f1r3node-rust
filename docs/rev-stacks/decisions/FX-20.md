# FX-20: the close is recorded, not charged, until B6 (Decision 119)

Base: `patch/FX-8b` at b2bc4a75e.

## What changed

- **κ in the record.** `BlockRecordProto` field 20, `close_phlo`, holds κ: the close's COMM cells and the work of the close's own PoS code, as the comm ledger meters them when the close runs (the `None`-surface cells and work). `BlockRecord::with_close_phlo` and `close_phlo()` carry it.
- **Replay recomputes κ.** `replay_after` reads κ from the replayed close and rejects a record whose κ differs ("the close's cost"). Nothing is charged from κ.
- **Removed:** `v6/close_charge.rs` (`CloseCharge`, the pre-charge, the shard-floor price, the consensus source and its cache), the gate's close hold (`hold_close`, `gate_block_with_close`, `check_recorded_with_close`, `GateOutcome::close_hold`), the `close_precharge` parameter of `execute_held` and `replay_held`, `RuntimeOps::close_charge`, `close_payer`, `charge_close`, the close fingerprint in the replay cache key (dev's `replay_payload_hash` is back), the void-(a) predicate, and the tests of these.
- **Reason (a):** the variant `VoidReason::CloseUncovered` is removed. `RESERVED_CLOSE_UNCOVERED = 1` reserves the tag. `VoidReason::from_tag(1)` is an error, so a record or a void-block evidence with tag 1 does not decode. A comment marks the tag as reserved for B6.
- **Kept:** void reasons (b), (c) and (d) and their tests, the traced (d) close, Decisions 101 and 109, the linear PoS close, lane B's epoch fix. Continuations that the close fires keep their gating against their installer (Decision 120) until lane C's FX-19.

## Decisions

### D1. A void record has κ = 0

- **Decision:** a record voided before its runs, (b) or (c), and a (d) record carry κ = 0. `from_proto` refuses a void record with κ ≠ 0. `with_failed_close` sets κ to 0.
- **Alternatives:** record the phlo that the failed close used before it failed.
- **Reason:** (b) and (c) run no close. A failed close stops at a point that the replay of the rigged trace does not meter the same way, so its partial cost is not a stable value to check. B6 decides whether a failed close is charged.
- **Reversible:** yes.

### D2. κ leaves the runtime through a shared cell in `SystemGate`

- **Decision:** `SystemGate::close_phlo` is an `Arc<Mutex<Option<u64>>>`. The runtime writes κ when a `CloseBlockDeploy` ends. The executor reads it after the close.
- **Alternatives:** add κ to the return type of `compute_state_gated` and `replay_compute_state_gated`.
- **Reason:** the return types are dev's and have many callers. The cell changes no dev signature.
- **Reversible:** yes.

### D3. The measured bound stays in `close_bound_spec`

- **Decision:** the test keeps 200,000 + 13,000 per bond as a local bound on κ.
- **Alternatives:** remove the bound tests with the pre-charge.
- **Reason:** the measurements are data for B6, and they now check κ directly.
- **Reversible:** yes.

## Decision 88 labels

- *Known issue:* the refusal event and the close's funding meter stay until FX-19. Reason: Decision 120 keeps the interim gating. The removal is agreed in `memos/FX-19-record-format.md`.

### D4. The cross-host fixture is written again

- **Decision:** `tests/fixtures/v6/cross_host` is written again with `write_cross_host_fixture`. The old `record.bin` has no κ, so its replay fails with "the close's cost".
- **Alternatives:** none that keep the check. A record without κ must not replay.
- **Reason:** field 20 is part of the record.
- **Reversible:** yes.
