- `rholang/tests/seal_wiring_spec.rs::code_with_no_record_draws_on_no_wallet_under_seal_records`: a paying contract stored with records off moves nothing when Alice fires it with records on. With records off, it pays.
- `casper/tests/util/rholang/system_vault_v6_test.rs`: the facade runs with records on. `the_facade_has_no_transfer` sends a facade `transfer`, gets no answer, and moves nothing. The facade benchmark is removed.
- `casper/tests/v6/genesis_contracts_records_spec.rs`, through the executor with records on:
  - ListOps, Either, NonNegativeNumber, MakeMint, AuthKey, Stack, TokenMetadata, TreeHashMap and the registry each answer;
  - the facade's `find` and `balance` answer, and `transfer` does not;
  - every PoSV6 entry point answers. `withdraw`, `commitRandomImage` and `revealRandom` with a deployer id are refused with "Unknown validator handle". `bond` registers.
  - Fs is not covered. It never calls `rho:rev`, and it has no fixed lookup name.
- `casper/tests/v6/pos_bond_admin_spec.rs`. Every block runs through `BlockExecutor` and replays to its post-root.
  - `a_withdrawn_bond_is_released_to_its_validator_after_quarantine`: the FX-10 test, with the native bond, the pending registration and a withdrawal by handle.
  - `two_pos_bonds_on_one_native_bond_admit_the_first_and_release_the_rest`: a native bond of 1,500 backs two PoS bonds of 1,000. Exactly one is admitted. The other is dropped, the native bond falls to 1,000, and 500 goes back to the wallet. The order of the two pending bonds inside one deploy is the order of their COMMs, so the test accepts either one as the first.
  - `a_pending_bond_backed_only_by_a_withdrawn_bond_is_dropped`: Alice bonds 1,000 and withdraws it, and it goes into quarantine. A new PoS bond with no new native bond is dropped, and nothing is released. This is the "withdrawn before the close" case. A user cannot release a native bond directly, so the bond leaves through PoS's own withdrawal.
  - `a_published_deployer_id_does_not_act_for_its_validator`: Mallory holds Alice's published deployer id. Her `withdraw`, commit and reveal are each refused with "Unknown validator handle", and Alice stays bonded. Alice commits and reveals with her handle.
- `gated_executor_spec::a_user_transfer_and_bond_survive_play_and_replay` uses the native bond and the pending registration, and checks that the handle arrives.

## Open items

1. **Handle custody (authority).** A deploy cannot name an unforgeable name in its source or its parameters. So a validator's later deploy cannot use the handle, except through code that the bonding deploy left. The tests keep the handle on a public test channel, which is not safe outside a test. Options are in the memo: (A) a custody contract that checks a secp256k1 signature, (B) a use that the bonding deploy fixes in advance, and (C) a native handle process that checks the seal.
2. **Bond hijack.** `bond` reads the key from a deployer id. A third party with Alice's published id can register a pending bond for her. If it is admitted before Alice's own, the third party gets the handle. With it, the third party can withdraw her bond (the REV goes back to Alice) or commit and reveal for her. Options (A) and (C) of item 1 also close this.
3. **Genesis validators** have no bonding deploy, so they have no handle and cannot withdraw, commit or reveal.
