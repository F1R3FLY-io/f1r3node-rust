# P3-07b decisions: token kinds as located stacks

Patch P3-07b, rev-stacks patch plan r7.10, lane C. Branch `patch/P3-07b`, cut
from `patch/P2-integration` (`3b5a7e90a`). This patch replaces the separate
kind-wallet machinery of P3-07 (decision 3 of `P3-07.md`).

**Review:** lane B reviews the journal change (`rev_stack/journal.rs`).

## Rulings used

- Decision 46 (the ruling on memo H): token kinds are instances of the one
  located-stack mechanism. Wallets are keyed by owner and kind. The journal
  has one entry type, parametric in kind. Mints are marked and are allowed
  only for the holder of the kind's name. Conservation is checked per kind.
  REV is checked against the genesis supply. Kind stacks go through the
  journal, so that they survive the v6 merge.
- Design r8, P9: cost accounting is one mechanism, parametric in token kind.
  REV differs from another kind only by policy. Genesis holds the REV kind
  name, REV is the only staking token, and `phloPrice` is in REV.
- Design r8, P10: authority over a stack is named in the term.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | `TokenKind` is the name of a kind: the id bytes of an unforgeable name. REV is the kind `Blake2b256("f1r3fly/token-kind/rev/v1")` (`TokenKind::rev`). | A `Rev` enum variant. | P9: REV is a kind like any other. Only policy makes it special. No URN or term produces that id, so only genesis holds it. | Yes |
| 2 | A wallet is located at `(owner, kind)`. `wallet_channel_id_of(o, REV)` is the P0 wallet channel `Blake2b256("f1r3fly/rev-stack/wallet/v1" ‖ canonical(o))`. For another kind `k` it is `Blake2b256("f1r3fly/rev-stack/wallet/v1" ‖ 0x10 ‖ len(k) ‖ k ‖ canonical(o))`. The tag `0x10` is not an owner tag, so the two preimage sets do not meet. | A new domain for all kinds, REV included. | The REV case keeps every existing wallet channel, so genesis state and recorded blocks stay valid. | Until a shard holds tokens of a kind |
| 3 | `RevStacks` carries a kind (`RevStacks::new` is REV, `of_kind` selects another). Every operation (deposit, spend, transfer, combine, balance, the stripe locks) is the same code for every kind. | — | One mechanism (P9). | Yes |
| 4 | `kind_owner`, `TOKEN_KIND_DOMAIN` and the reducer's `eval_kind_stacks` are removed. The reducer has `pay_stacks(kind, ...)` for REV stacks and kind transfers, and `mint_stacks(kind, ...)` for mints. | — | Decision 46: no separate kind-wallet machinery. | Yes |
| 5 | `JournalEntry` is `{ kind, from: Source, to, amount }` with `Source::Mint` or `Source::Wallet(owner)`. On the journal channel a transfer is `["transfer", seq, kind, from, to, amount]` and a mint is `["mint", seq, kind, to, amount]`. | A separate mint journal. | One entry type, with the mint marked by its tag. | Yes |
| 6 | `JournalEntry::check` refuses a REV mint (`RevStackError::RevMint`). `record`, `journaled_transfer` and the decoder `entries` all call it. Settlement refuses a REV mint again in `apply` and in `settle_block`, and `Settlement::from_proto` refuses a recorded one. | One check only. | No term can mint REV, and a forged journal or a forged block record cannot either. | Yes |
| 7 | A mint is allowed only for the holder of the kind's name: the reducer mints only when the stack's signature, after substitution, is the unforgeable name `k` itself (P3-07, decision 2). Only a holder of `k` can write `k`. The public name `bundle0{*k}` gives a transfer, never a mint. | A stored issuer list. | The authority is the name in the term (P10). | Yes |
| 8 | The journal nets per `(owner, kind)`. In a run, a kind transfer is checked against the kind balance plus the run's journaled credits less its journaled debits. Reservations hold REV only, so `held` is 0 for another kind. | — | Same rule as REV (P2-21). | Yes |
| 9 | The merge treats a journaled debit of `(owner, kind)` as a payer of the wallet channel at `(owner, kind)`. A mint debits no wallet and is no payer. | Payers by owner only. | Two runs that debit different kinds of one owner do not conflict. | Yes |
| 10 | Settlement applies each kept journal entry in its kind: a transfer moves between `(from, kind)` and `(to, kind)`, and a mint deposits on `(to, kind)`. | — | Kind stacks then reach the settled root (Q-29 fix of P2-21). | Yes |
| 11 | Conservation is checked per kind over the touched wallets: after = before + minted. For REV, minted must be 0, so the touched REV wallets hold the same total before and after. With this, the audit `held + legacy_bonds = genesis supply` (`settle::audit`) stays true. | A whole-state sum per kind. | The check covers every wallet that settlement writes. | Yes |
| 12 | `TransferProto` gets `kind` (field 5, bytes) and `mint` (field 6, bool). An empty `kind` is REV, so records written before this patch decode unchanged. A mint has an empty `from`. | — | Compatible with recorded settlements. | Yes |
| 13 | The REV history (`v6::history::transfers`) lists REV wallet-to-wallet transfers only. | Show kind moves as REV moves. | A kind move is not a REV move. | Yes |
| 14 | `kindBalance` reads the `(owner, kind)` wallet through the same code as `balance`, and adds the open journal's net for that kind. | — | One mechanism. | Yes |

## P10 notes for lane A1

The source of a REV stack and of a kind transfer is still the deployer
(`deploy_data.deployer_id`), as in P2-19. That is authority from execution
context, which P10 does not allow. This patch does not change it. Lane A1 is
auditing this point, and the fix will follow its findings.
