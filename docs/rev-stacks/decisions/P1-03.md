## Implementation decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Reason | Reversible |
|---|----------|--------------|--------|------------|
| 1 | The shard resource policy is stored on chain as a `TokenMetadata("resourcePolicy", ret)` getter that returns the encoded policy, as in 5b006ce52. | A separate registry entry or contract. | Keeps the source's location, and the template placeholder renders as an empty string on a legacy genesis, so the legacy genesis term is unchanged. | Yes |
| 2 | Policy encoding: one version byte (1), then the four fields as big-endian 64-bit words (block records, block record bytes, maximum envelopes, demand cap); 33 bytes. Decoding checks the length, the version and the policy's own checks. | JSON; prost. | A fixed, canonical byte form for a value that enters the genesis state root. | Yes, before any shard launches |
| 3 | `Genesis::create_genesis_block_with_policy(rm, genesis, policy)` is the protocol-6 entry point. A policy requires `version == 6`, and `version == 6` requires a policy. `create_genesis_block` keeps dev's behaviour and passes no policy. The `Genesis` struct is unchanged. | Add a `resource_policy` field to `Genesis`. | A new field changes nine construction sites in other lanes' files. The switch wiring (memo B) will pass the configured policy to the new entry point. | Yes |
| 4 | `v6::genesis_policy::read_genesis_policy(rm, state_hash)` reads the policy back with an exploratory deploy, following dev's `token_metadata_check`. It lives in `v6/`, not in `RuntimeOps` / `RuntimeManager` as in 5b006ce52. | Port the `RuntimeOps` method. | Those files are outside P1-03's hotspot list. dev's public `play_exploratory_deploy` is enough. | Yes |
| 5 | The record-encoding switch of 5b006ce52 (legacy vs cost RSpace records, and the empty-state root that follows it) is not ported. | Port it. | It belongs to the costacc cost-record fields, and the r6 design relaxes byte identity with dev (Oct 29 decision). | Yes |
| 6 | Ordered envelopes stay as dev's `ProcessedDeploy` in unrun form (`ProcessedDeploy::empty`: cost 0, no log, not failed, no system error) carrying plain signed `DeployData`, as the ported module already proposes and validates them. No `ProcessedUserDeploy::Ordered` variant and no change to `models` or the protobuf files. | Port the `ProcessedUserDeploy` enum and `DeployEnvelope` of 14e800d18. | dev has no `ProcessedUserDeploy`. The enum carries costacc's offered-funded envelope. Plain `DeployData` is what the brief asks for. | Yes |
| 7 | `ordered_only` from 14e800d18 is not added. | Add the flag. | Every deploy in a v6 body must already be in unrun form, so a v6 body is always ordered-only. | Yes |
| 8 | The refusal of ordered envelopes in dev's deploy-running replay path (`interpreter_util.rs` in 14e800d18) is deferred to the hook round of P1-01. | Add it now. | It routes v6 blocks away from the legacy replay, which is a hook point. Until then no v6 block reaches that path. | Yes |
| 9 | The gate change in 14e800d18 is not ported. | — | The gate (P2-04) is not on this branch. | Yes |
| 10 | P1-03 adds no protobuf fields. On `feature/cost-accounted-rho`, P2-18 uses `Par` fields 13 and 14 (RhoTypes.proto) and `DeployDataProto` field 18 (CasperMessage.proto). These stay free. | — | For the lane C agent to confirm. | Yes |

## Tests

- `v6::resource_policy`: the encoding round-trips and malformed encodings are refused.
- `v6::validity`: ordered envelopes round-trip through the block wire format and pass the structural check; an executed or failed envelope is refused; the policy bounds the envelope count and the demand.
- `casper/tests/genesis/v6_genesis_spec.rs`: a protocol-6 genesis builds and its policy reads back from the post-state; a legacy genesis has no policy; a policy without version 6, version 6 without a policy and an invalid policy are refused.
