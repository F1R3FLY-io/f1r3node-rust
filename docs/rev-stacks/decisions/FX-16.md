# FX-16: the close bound (lane B)

Decisions 94, 96, 97, 98. Plan r8.16. Scope now: measure the worst-case work of the close's PoS code and choose the bound. The pre-charge applies at execution, and an uncovered close voids the block. The mechanism waits for Q-33.

## Measurement

Method: a v6 block executed and replayed by `BlockExecutor` on a protocol-6 genesis, with the close's undecorated cells and work charged to the proposer at price 1. The proposer's debit, less its settlement credit, is the close's phlo. Release build. Each row is one block after genesis.

| Validators | Epoch length | Pending bonds in the block | Close phlo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1000 (no boundary) | 0 | 60,800 |
| 1 | 1 (boundary) | 0 | 139,737 |
| 10 | 1 | 0 | 247,462 |
| 100 | 1000 | 0 | 182,040 |
| 100 | 1 | 0 | 4,400,917 |
| 100 | 1 | 1 | 4,874,406 |
| 100 | 1 | 16 offered, 1 merged | 4,874,460 |
| 100 | 1 | 64 offered, 1 merged | 4,874,439 |

- 100 validators is the full active set of the test genesis (`number_of_active_validators` = 100).
- The epoch boundary dominates: `pickActiveValidators` sorts all bonds, so the close is about 25 times dearer at a boundary with 100 bonds.
- Pending bonds: the bond registrations of a block all write the PoS state cell, so only one of them merges. Each block's close settles at most one new bond.
- Slashing: a v6 block carries no slash system deploy (`require_no_slashes`), so the v6 close runs no slashing code. There is nothing to measure.
- Not measured: an epoch boundary that releases many quarantined withdrawers at once. Each release calls `RevBondAdmin!("release", …)`. This needs a multi-block test with withdrawals and is the next measurement.

## Bound

**`CLOSE_PHLO_BOUND` = 10,000,000 phlo**, about 2.05 times the worst measured close (4,874,544).

- The pre-charge is the bound times the price. The price is 1 until Q-33 says where the proposer's price comes from. A price must be recorded or fixed in consensus, so that replay gives the same charge.
- The test `close_bound_spec::the_close_stays_inside_its_bound` runs each row above and fails if a close passes the bound. It lands with the mechanism, because it measures through the close charge.

## Findings (Decision 88 labels)

1. **The close's work at a boundary grows with the number of bonds, not only the active set.** `pickActiveValidators` runs over `allBonds`. Anyone can bond with `minimum_bond`, so the bonded set, and so the close's work, has no fixed limit. A fixed bound holds only while the bonded set stays near the measured size. **Known issue, not blocking:** the charge never passes the pre-charge, nobody loses funds, and each extra bond locks `minimum_bond`. The fix needs a ruling: a cap on the bonded set, or a bound that scales with it (for example, a fixed part plus a part for each bond).
2. **Substitution into a fired continuation is charged to the firer.** With the same bond, a larger continuation body for the handle raised the proposer's close charge by 291 phlo (180,160 to 180,451). The installer's own charge also rose. The likely cause is the cost of substituting the received data into the continuation body, which runs before the body enters the installer's membrane. **Known issue, not blocking:** the amount is bounded by the size of a body that the installer paid to store. For lane C to confirm, because Decision 94 gives a fired continuation's cells and work to its installer.

## Revision 2: Decisions 102 and 106, and the per-bond measurement

Decision 102 (F47): the pre-charge is a base amount plus a per-bond rate, applied to the bonds at the attested root plus one for each block of lag. Decision 106: the close is priced at the shard's price floor. Lane A2 owns the void mechanism in FX-17. This revision keeps only the parts that fold into it.

### Measurement: the close against the number of bonds

Same method as above, with every validator funded, and the charge cap lifted for the measurement. The epoch fix of FX-17 (`patch/FX-17-epoch`, 214571b2e) is merged in.

| Bonds | Close inside an epoch | Close at an epoch boundary (current PoS) | Close at an epoch boundary (native active-set pick) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 76,179 | 252,194 | 142,088 |
| 50 | 125,139 | 1,407,174 | 313,488 |
| 100 | 186,339 | 4,405,649 | 527,738 |
| 150 | 218,755 | 9,030,897 | 641,258 |
| 200 | 251,155 | over 10,000,000 (capped) | 754,708 |

- **Inside an epoch:** about 1,220 phlo for each bond up to 100 bonds, and about 650 above that.
- **At an epoch boundary, current PoS:** the growth is close to quadratic. A fit through 50, 100 and 150 bonds gives about 325·n² + 11,150·n + 36,000 phlo. The cause is `pickActiveValidators`. It runs `ListOps.map` over the bond list, and each step of that loop carries the list built so far.
- **At an epoch boundary, native pick:** with `pickActiveValidators` replaced by `allBonds.keys().toList().take(N).toSet()` (one expression, no loop), the growth is linear. The marginal cost is at most about 4,300 phlo for each bond below the active-set size, and about 2,300 above it. This experiment is on the local branch `lane-b/FX-16-measure` and is not pushed. It changes the PoS term, so it is not part of FX-16.

### Measurement: an epoch that releases many withdrawers

100 bonded validators, epoch length 1,000, quarantine 0. Validator i withdraws in block i. Block 1,000 then moves all pending withdrawers and releases them in one close.

| Released withdrawers | Current PoS | Native active-set pick |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 4,405,667 | 527,756 |
| 10 | 5,589,901 | 2,407,035 |
| 30 | 8,986,141 | 6,986,065 |

Each release costs about 190,000 to 230,000 phlo, and the marginal cost grows with the number released. A fit gives about 1,370·w² + 174,000·w on top of the boundary close. `movePendingWithdrawer`, `removeQuarantinedWithdrawers` and `computeRemove` fold over the withdrawers, and each fold step carries the whole PoS state. A release also calls `RevBondAdmin!("release", …)`.

### What this patch keeps for lane A2

- `v6::close_charge`:
  - `pre_charge_phlo(bonds, lag) = CLOSE_BASE_PHLO + CLOSE_PHLO_PER_BOND × (bonds + lag)`, and `pre_charge(bonds, lag, floor)` in REV.
  - `CloseCharge { price, pre_charge }`. Its `charge(close_phlo, balance)` is the actual cells and work at the floor price, capped at the pre-charge and at the balance.
  - `shard_floor(runtime_manager, root)` reads the floor from PoS (`getConsensusParameters`, the same source that `casper.rs` adopts at boot).
- The close charge is per call. `RuntimeManager::compute_state_closing` and `replay_compute_state_closing` take an `Option<CloseCharge>`. `compute_state` and `replay_compute_state` pass `None` and do not change. `BlockExecutor::with_close_charge` passes it into play and replay. The earlier shared switch on `RuntimeManager` is removed.
- The replay cache key includes the close charge (its price and pre-charge), so a cached replay of an uncharged close is never used for a charged one.
- The undecorated cells and work of the close (the `None` key of the ledger) are charged to the block sender's wallet and paid into the compute wallet, after the close, inside the post-state root. Stored continuations that the close fires stay with their installers (D8, lane C's `CloseFunding`).
- Genesis runs no close, so it charges nobody.
- The gate hold, the cover checks at acceptance and at `create_block`, and the funded validators in the v6 test network are removed. They belong to lane A2's void mechanism.

### Constants (proposed)

`CLOSE_BASE_PHLO` = 200,000 and `CLOSE_PHLO_PER_BOND` = 9,000. These are about twice the native-pick measurement: about 100,000 at 0 bonds, and at most about 4,300 for each bond. With the current PoS, they do not bound an epoch close above about 20 bonds. The bound test is therefore ignored until the ruling below.

### Findings (Decision 88 labels)

1. **Blocking: a linear pre-charge cannot bound the close with the current PoS term.** The epoch close grows about as 325·n² in the bonds, and the release of w withdrawers grows about as 1,370·w². Under a linear pre-charge, the work above the cap runs unpaid. That diverges from r12 §6.3: all Rholang is charged, and no step runs unpaid. Proposed resolution, in order of preference:
   1. Make the PoS close linear. Use the native active-set pick measured above (one expression). Rewrite the withdrawer folds to touch only the withdrawer maps, not the whole state. Then the per-bond rate holds.
   2. Release at most K withdrawers in each close. The rest wait for the next close, as the epoch fix of FX-17 permits. This bounds the release term to K times the release cost.
   3. A quadratic term in the pre-charge. This is not recommended: the pre-charge becomes large for any shard with many bonds.
2. **Known issue, not blocking: a shard floor of 0 makes the close free.** Decision 106 prices the close at the floor. The test genesis and the default local configuration use 0.
3. The earlier finding that the close's work grows with the bonded set is now covered by Decision 102.

### Re-measurement: withdrawers

100 validators, epoch length 1,000, quarantine 0. Validator V_i withdraws in block B_i. Epoch 1 is the close of B1000. Epoch 2 is the close of B2000. "Bonds" is the number of bonds at the pre-state of that close.

| Withdrawers | Epoch 1 (bonds) | Epoch 1 phlo | Epoch 2 (bonds) | Epoch 2 phlo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 527,907 | 100 | 527,907 |
| 10 | 100 | 1,714,142 | 90 | 784,879 |
| 30 | 100 | 4,503,022 | 70 | 1,530,519 |
| 60 | 100 | 10,586,074 | 40 | 3,998,979 |
| 99 | 100 | 21,901,997 | 1 | 9,629,593 |

**Finding — blocking (the same §6.3 divergence as revision 2, narrowed):** the release cap does not bound the epoch close when many validators withdraw in one epoch.
- **Epoch 1:** `movePendingWithdrawer` moves every pending withdrawer in a fold that carries the whole PoS state. Each move costs about 120,000 phlo at 100 bonds. With 10 withdrawers, the close passes the pre-charge (1,714,142 > 1,100,000).
- **Epoch 2:** the withdrawers that the cap held back are no longer in `allBonds`, so the pre-charge does not count them. `ListOps.filter` still walks them, and the state that each fold step carries still holds them. With 99 withdrawers and 1 bond left, the close costs 9.6M phlo against a pre-charge of 209,000.
- **Proposed resolution** (`docs/rev-stacks/memos/D107-loops.md`):
  1. The withdrawer rewrite, about 2.5 days.
  2. Or, until then:
     - cap the moves of one close as well;
     - limit the quarantine filter to a fixed window;
     - count withdrawers in quarantine as bonds for the pre-charge.

     These caps slow withdrawals: with a cap of 1, 100 withdrawals take 100 epochs.

`close_bound_spec::an_epoch_with_many_withdrawers_stays_inside_its_pre_charge` asserts the bound for 10 and 30 withdrawers. It is ignored until one of the two resolutions lands.
