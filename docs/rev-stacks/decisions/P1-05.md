## How 0028 applied

`git apply -3` of 3500a5726 onto `patch/P1-04b`:

- Clean: `evidence.rs`, `slash.rs`, `store.rs`, `validators.rs` (new) and `record.rs` (`footprint()`).
- `core.rs`: 0028's version is taken whole. Its conflicts were with cdc23effd's record retention, which 0028's `RecordStore` replaces. 0028's own retention (`MemoryRecordStore`, `Retention`) is kept: it belongs to 0028, not to P1-13.
- `engine.rs`: 0028's version (`V6Parts`, `with_parts`, evidence sink, validator source, record store) with the P1-04b adaptations reapplied: no gate, no offered pool, plain `DeployData` selection, dev's mutex-free `KeyValueDeployStorage` (as on M0), and `V6Engine::new` with the signature the P1-01 hooks call. `quarantine` (the offer rejection log of cdc23effd) is left out with the gate.
- `executor.rs`: the P1-04b `BlockExecutor` with 0028's slash hook added: a `SlashSource` (default `NoSlashing`), `with_slash_source`, and `require_no_slashes` before execute and before replay. Replay also refuses a record that names another block, as in 0028.
- `mod.rs`: 0028's new modules beside P1-04b's.
- `casper/tests/protocol_v6_genesis_spec.rs` does not exist on this base. Its one new test (the attested-root validator source reads the floor committee) is ported into `casper/tests/genesis/v6_genesis_spec.rs`, on the P1-03 protocol-6 genesis.
