# Q-32: where work under a membrane t stops, in play and in replay

Lanes C and A2, 2026-11-03. Base: `patch/D76` at 1502a4e98. Lane A2 wrote the replay side. Lane C must confirm the play side (the meter).

## The problem

Each branch of a `Par` runs as its own task (`spawn_in_dispatch_scope`, five sites in `reduce.rs`). The order of steps under t is therefore not fixed. Under Decision 86, t stops at the first step that it cannot pay, and its completed work stands. Replay today forces only the logged COMMs. A produce or consume that finds no logged COMM is stored. So a replay that continues t past the play stop point stores different items, and the user-phase root does not agree. Replay tolerates unused COMMs only for a failed evaluation (`check_replay_data_with_fix`). A stop under t is not a failure.

If t does not stop, nothing changes. The same COMMs fire, so the same steps run, and the total charge to t is the same in any order.

## Option (i): a fixed evaluation order

All work under t runs in one fixed order, so the meter stops t at the same step in play and replay. Nothing goes in the record.

- **Not sufficient for t alone.** A COMM body under t starts when its partner arrives. The partner can run outside t. A fixed order under t therefore needs a fixed order for the whole run, which makes the reducer sequential for each run.
- **Compute between COMMs:** charged step by step, the same in play and replay.
- **Cost:** a change to the reducer schedule at all spawn sites and in the deferred-dispatch scope. Estimate 3 to 5 days, plus a re-audit. **High** risk: the scheduler is where the races came from. Each run also loses its internal parallelism.

## Option (ii): the record gives the stop, and replay enforces it

For each membrane t that stops in a run, the record entry gets `{t (owner and origin, Decision 80), D_t (t's drawn total), c (the cost of the refused step)}`.

Play: the meter admits each step against t's available balance A_t (the pre-state balance, less what the block holds, less what t drew earlier in the run). At the first step that it cannot pay, it records D_t and c and stops t.

Replay:
1. Forces the logged COMMs, as now.
2. Under a stopped t, refuses each produce or consume that is not a logged event. The refusal is replay's stop. The datum and the continuation carry their records (D79), so the membrane of each event is known at the storage boundary.
3. Meters t's work without a stop, as W_t. Without IO, a branch terminates, so step 2 ends each branch.
4. Accepts the stop only if `A_t − c < D_t ≤ A_t` and `D_t ≤ W_t`. Each step that play admitted also runs in replay, so an honest record always passes. A proposer cannot stop t early or charge t for work that did not occur. The only freedom is less than one step, inside t's own wallet.
5. Charges t exactly D_t, and requires that no logged event under t is left unused (strict, not the failed-evaluation tolerance).

- **Compute between COMMs:** play admits them step by step. Replay bounds them with W_t and does not reproduce them one by one. Only t pays. The client pays nothing for t's work.
- **Completed work under t:** these are exactly the logged events. They stand, and D_t pays for them.
- **Decision 48:** D_t is one draw on t's wallet in the run's journal. The close check and the block-wide check see it as any other draw and do not change. A_t uses the same terms as the admission test.
- **Cost:** play-side stop marker, 0.5 day (lane C, on the meter that both options need). Replay, 1 day (lane A2). Record field and interleaving tests, 0.5 day. Total 1.5 to 2 days, inside FX-15. **Medium** risk: new code is at the storage boundary in replay. The scheduler does not change.

## Recommendation

**(ii).** It keeps the scheduler, uses the replay mechanism that already forces COMMs, and fits the Friday handoff. Option (i) would need a sequential reducer for each run, at about twice the cost and with the risk where the races have been.

The FX-15 tests must show that play under 1, 2 and 4 workers and replay agree on the user-phase root and on D_t. They must also show that a record which stops t early, or charges more than W_t, is refused.

No new finding. The analysis did not find an issue for a Decision 88 label.
